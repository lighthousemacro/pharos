02The four pieces, plainly

The market

the venue, on Arc

A program on a blockchain that holds money. An automated bookmaker for one yes/no question. It escrows USDC, quotes odds that move as money comes in (the "crowd price"), and pays each winning share exactly 1 USDC.

1 USDC in → mints 1 YES + 1 NO
exactly one side wins, pays 1 USDC
⇒ payout == collateral. always. it cannot go bust.

The pricing engine

the moat

The only hard-to-copy piece, and the reason to do this. It reads your DB and answers one question per market: what is the probability this print clears this number. Your research desk, turned into an automated line-setter. Detail in section 03.

The agents

what the hackathon judges

Three small autonomous programs, no human runs them. One opens and prices markets ahead of each release. One resolves them on the official government number. One conducts the other two.

The front-end

what people see

A dashboard in your 23/89/BB palette. Each market is a card with one gauge showing both prices, the edge, the framework's reasoning, and the signal-quality readout.

03The moat, in detail

How "US headline CPI trend ≥ 3.0%" gets its framework probability. Real numbers from your DB.

1
Point-in-time slice
Only data that was public on the day the market opened. Publication-lagged. This is your backtest discipline, enforced.
no look-ahead
2
Consistent trend nowcast
Blend of 3m/3m and 6m/6m seasonally-adjusted annualized rates. Not a single noisy print, a de-noised trend.
μ ≈ 5.30%
3
Pillar signal
Inflation Heat, normalized into a clean sequential z-score using only past information.
+0.02σ
4
Calibration
How well has this pillar actually predicted this print, historically? Fit on aligned history, not hand-picked.
IC +0.30 · n=241
5
Conviction shrink
Trust the tilt in proportion to proven skill. Strong edge, full conviction. Weak edge, pull back toward 50%.
→ 83.2% YES

The part that makes it credible: it knows when it does not know

The conviction shrink is your own position sizing (Base × Conviction × Regime) applied to the model itself. It only expresses a strong view when the history earns it. Watch it, live:

CPI / Inflation Heat
IC +0.30 → conviction 1.00 → 83.2%  committed
GDP / Activity Pulse
IC +0.01 → conviction 0.04 → 50.5%  abstains

The GDP market is the proof it is honest. That pillar has no demonstrated edge on that print, so the model refuses to fake a view. A model that abstains when it lacks an edge is the credible one. That single behavior is the answer to "is this real or hand-waved."

Methodology lineage: point-in-time information states, consistent trends, zn-scores and signal-return calibration come from the Macrosynergy / JPMaQS quantamental tradition. Those documents are proprietary and confidential, so the concepts (also public in their free Academy) are applied to your data and framework. No Macrosynergy text, tickers, dataset or package is in the repo. We can say "quantamental tradition." We cannot say "powered by JPMaQS," and we are not.

08The hard questions, and the answers

Isn't this just Polymarket?
No. They are a venue with no price. We are a venue with a model. The contract is a commodity. The price is the product.
Can't a team fork your contracts?
Yes, in an afternoon, and it gets them nothing. The moat is the framework and the data behind the quote, not the AMM.
Is the model real or hand-waved?
Real, and it abstains when it lacks an edge. Show the GDP market shrinking to 50%. A model that knows when it does not know is the credible one.
Who resolves it?
An agent, on official BLS/BEA/Fed data, using the same calculation it priced with. Not a human opinion.
Why Arc specifically?
USDC-denominated gas (no buying a separate gas token), instant USDC settlement, and an encrypted mempool so a large macro bet does not leak its intent. The last one is the institutional wedge.
Can it rug or go insolvent?
No. Every dollar in mints one YES and one NO, exactly one side wins, payout equals collateral by construction. Proven in tests.
Where do users come from?
Your existing audience already argues about these exact prints weekly. The market is the call to action under the research, and every resolved market is a content unit.
